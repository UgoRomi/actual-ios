About the app

Actual Native is an unofficial iPhone app for Actual Budget, open-source envelope budgeting software. It runs Actual's own engine on your device, so budgets, targets, rules and schedules behave as they do in Actual web and desktop. It is developed independently by Ugo Romi and is not affiliated with or endorsed by the Actual Budget project.

Getting started

  • Try the demo. Open the demo budget to explore the app without a server. It stays on your device.
  • Connect your server. To use your own budget, you need an Actual server, run by you or someone you trust. Enter its address, sign in with your server password or OpenID, and download a budget.
  • New to Actual? The Actual Budget documentation explains how to set up a server and how envelope budgeting works.

Common questions

The app can't connect to my server. Check that the address is correct and reachable from your iPhone, and that the server uses HTTPS with a valid certificate. Self-signed certificates and plain HTTP are not supported. Keeping your server on a recent version of Actual also helps.

My budget asks for a password I don't recognize. Budgets that use Actual's end-to-end encryption need their encryption password, which is separate from your server password.

Bank refresh doesn't work. The app uses the bank connections already set up on your Actual server. Set up or repair the connection in Actual web or desktop, then pull to refresh on the Accounts screen.

My widget looks out of date. iOS decides when apps refresh in the background. Opening the app syncs your budget and updates the widget straight away.

How do I remove a budget from my iPhone? Touch and hold it in the budget list. A budget on your server stays there and can be downloaded again, but changes not yet synced are lost.

Something works differently from Actual web. A few features, such as custom reports, are still only available in Actual web and desktop. The app notes where this applies.

Get help

For anything else, including bug reports and feature requests, open an issue on GitHub:

Open a GitHub issue

To help us fix a problem quickly, please include:

  • Your iPhone model and iOS version.
  • The app version, shown in the version history on the app's App Store page.
  • Your Actual server version, if you use one.
  • What you did, what you expected, and what happened instead. Screenshots help.

Issues are public, so please don't share passwords, server addresses, or budget details you'd rather keep private.
